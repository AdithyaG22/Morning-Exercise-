# How Morning Move orders a workout

The planner (`buildPlan` in `js/plan.js`) picks exercises for your level and focus, then puts them
in an order based on common coaching and exercise-science guidance. This page explains the rules and
where they come from.

## The rules

**1. Warm-up: RAMP (Raise → Activate/Mobilise → build up)**
- The first move is always a gentle **pulse raiser** (March in Place, Step Jacks, Arm Swings or Shadow
  Boxing) when your focus allows it.
- Then **moving** mobility exercises, from gentlest to most active: arm and hip circles, twists,
  side bends.
- **No held stretches in the warm-up.** Long static holds just before effort can slightly reduce strength
  and power, while moving (dynamic) warm-ups help.

**2. Main block: a natural flow**
- **Standing first, then down to the floor once**, in the order standing → plank/face-down → kneeling
  → side-lying → seated → lying on your back. You don't keep getting up and down.
- **Alternate body areas** (upper body, lower body, core) so one area recovers while another works.
- **Bigger multi-muscle moves early**, smaller ones later.
- **Intensity builds up, peaks about two-thirds of the way through, then eases off.**
- **No two very hard cardio moves back to back** (for example, burpees then jumping jacks) when it can
  be avoided.
- **Long sessions run as rounds (circuits).** If the session needs more moves than the pool has, each
  round uses every move once, in the order above, and the next round starts standing again.

**3. Yoga sequencing**
- A flowing warm-up (the Half Sun Salutation goes first when it's in the session), then standing poses,
  then floor poses in the same natural order as above.
- After a **backbend** (Cobra, Sphinx, Bridge), a **counter-pose** follows: Child's Pose after a
  face-down backbend, Knees to Chest after Bridge.

**4. Cool-down: lower the intensity, then hold stretches**
- Stretches are chosen for **the muscles you just worked**, and differ from the main block where
  possible.
- They continue from where the main block ended. If you finished on the floor, the floor stretches come
  first and you finish standing with Deep Breathing. If you finished standing, you start with Deep
  Breathing and end lying down.

## How well the planner follows them

Tested on 1,800 plans (every focus × level × 5/10/15/20/30 min × No jumping on/off × 12 days):

| Check | Old planner | New planner |
|---|---|---|
| Getting up off the floor mid-block (not counting new circuit rounds) | 746 times | 28 |
| Workout starts with a pulse raiser (where the focus allows one) | 21 % | 100 % |
| Same body area twice in a row (Full body) | 36 % of pairs | 25 % |
| Two hard cardio moves back to back (outside Cardio focus) | 91 | 41 |
| Yoga backbend without a counter-pose after it | 702 | 14 |
| Cool-down stretches target muscles just worked | 96 % | 100 % |
| Held stretches in the warm-up | 0 | 0 |
| Same exercise twice in a row | 0 | 0 |

The remaining misses come from small pools (e.g. Core focus is mostly core moves, Cardio with
No jumping has few standing moves) where the rules conflict and none can be fully met.

## Sources

Most sources are coaching guides and secondary summaries reached through web search, so treat them as
established practice rather than hard proof.

- RAMP warm-up (Jeffreys): [Science for Sport: warm-ups](https://www.scienceforsport.com/warm-ups/),
  [RAMP explained (club resource, PDF)](https://www.dunboynegaa.ie/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/RAMP.pdf)
- Static vs dynamic stretching before exercise (Behm et al. 2016 review):
  [Canadian Society for Exercise Physiology summary](https://csep.ca/2015/12/08/getting-the-most-from-your-stretching-routine/),
  [Memorial University summary](https://www.mun.ca/hkr/news-articles/getting-the-most-from-your-stretching-routine.php),
  [Frontiers in Physiology (2019)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physiology/articles/10.3389/fphys.2019.01468/pdf)
- Exercise order (large before small, multi-joint first; ACSM 2009): [IDEA Fit: does exercise order matter?](https://www.ideafit.com/does-exercise-order-really-matter-in-resistance-training),
  [PMC review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4327372)
- Alternating muscle groups / body parts in beginner circuits: [ACE: strength and cardio circuit for beginning clients](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/pros/expert-articles/5808/strength-and-cardio-circuit-for-beginning-clients/)
- Yoga sequencing (standing → peak → neutralise → cool-down, counter-poses after backbends):
  [Gymdesk yoga class template](https://gymdesk.com/blog/yoga-class-template),
  [Sri Sri School of Yoga: sequencing tips](https://srisrischoolofyoga.org/na/blog/11-essential-tips-for-sequencing-a-yoga-class/),
  [Iyengar NYC: Skillful Sequencing (PDF)](https://iyengarnyc.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/Fit_Yoga_Skillful_Sequencing.pdf)
- Cool-down (gradually lower intensity, then hold stretches 10–30 s): [Healthline: cooldown exercises](https://www.healthline.com/health/exercise-fitness/cooldown-exercises),
  [PureGym: cool-down guide](https://puregym.com/blog/the-beginner-s-guide-to-cool-down-exercises),
  [University of Nevada Extension](https://extension.unr.edu/4h/pub.aspx?PubID=3263)
