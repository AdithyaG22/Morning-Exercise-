## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Sitting at a laptop all day? Morning Move gives you a short, free workout every morning, guided by a 3D trainer you simply copy.

FOLLOW A 3D TRAINER
• The trainer demonstrates every exercise, so you never have to guess the form.
• It is sized to your height and weight.
• Turn the trainer around with your finger to see any exercise from every side.

SEE THE MUSCLES YOU WORK
• The muscles used in each exercise glow red while you work.
• During rest, the next exercise's muscles light up so you know what's coming.

SIMPLE INTERVAL WORKOUTS
• 20 seconds of exercise, 10 seconds of rest. You can change both.
• Choose a workout from 5 to 30 minutes.
• Voice coach and countdown beeps, so you don't have to watch the screen.
• The screen stays on during your workout.

FOR EVERY LEVEL
• Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced plans.
• Focus on Full body, Cardio, Core & back, Yoga, or Desk relief (stretches for people who sit all day).
• Ready-made routines, like the Beginner Full Body routine: 4 circuits with breathing breaks.
• 90 exercises and yoga poses, from jumping jacks and squats to cobra and downward dog.

TRACK YOUR HABIT
• Daily streak, weekly calendar, minutes trained and estimated calories.

PRIVATE AND LIGHT
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your phone.
• Works offline and takes up very little space.
• Light and dark themes.

Morning Move is about staying fit and healthy, not bodybuilding. It gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice. If you have an injury or a health condition, check with a doctor first.
