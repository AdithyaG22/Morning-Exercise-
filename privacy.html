Privacy policy

Morning Move · Last updated 10 October 2026

Morning Move is a free workout app. It does not collect, send or sell any personal data.

What the app stores

To size the 3D trainer and show your progress, the app saves the following only on your own device (in your browser's local storage):

  • your height, weight and chosen body type;
  • your workout settings (duration, level, focus, sound, theme);
  • your workout history (date, minutes, estimated calories, number of exercises).

This information never leaves your device. There are no accounts, no servers that receive your data, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app's or browser's storage, deletes everything it saved.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permissions. During a workout it may keep the screen awake and use your device's built-in voice to read out exercise names. Neither involves sending data anywhere.

Children

Morning Move is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Health notice

Morning Move gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice. If you have an injury or a health condition, check with a doctor before exercising, and stop any exercise that causes pain.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
